# Shisa: which layers make up each expression (see ../README.md).
# Started as a copy of Hachiware's layers (Shisa is built from Hachiware's model), to repaint as Shisa: the
# plush has big round eyes, thick orange flame-shaped brows and a wavy cat mouth. The mane is the model's.
# cheeks.png shows on every face.
Neutral: brows 1 + eyes open + mouth closed
Smile: brows 1 + eyes open + mouth half-open
Happy: brows 1 + eyes open + mouth open
Determined: brows 2 + eyes open + mouth closed
Worried: brows 3 + eyes open + mouth half-open + sweat
Teary: brows 3 + eyes open + mouth half-open + tears
Sobbing: brows 4 + eyes open + mouth half-open 2 + tears
Shocked: brows 4 + eyes open + mouth half-open 2
